# Medusa 1.9.2

"refresh" replaces "watch", and the phone now looks sources up and refreshes them too (IndexNode 0.10.3 does the same on the desktop).

- **Adding a source looks it up at once.** "Add to the index" and the share dialog write the source's job to `index/jobs/refresh/<sourceId>.json`, due now at priority 500, so the next free downloader (this phone included) lists it, whatever the schedule. The job body keeps the source's own priority, which the videos it finds inherit.
- **This phone claims refresh jobs.** The one worker takes downloads and source jobs together, in claim order (priority, then idx_added, then name), with yt-dlp's flat listing and no cookies, under the same rules as a download: control.json (a stopped or drained stage is skipped; the node stops only when both are), Wi-Fi and charging conditions, the spacing between jobs, and the one-hour back-off on a 429 or bot block. The daily cap and the per-IP cap only hold downloads back; a listing is one small request. A refresh does not count against the budget or the daily cap.
- **Lookup and refresh are different runs** (`run`, `lastRun`, `lastLookup`, `lastRefresh` in the job). A lookup is a full listing: every member goes to `index/sources/<id>/members.json` (id, title, length, channel, availability) and every video not yet known is queued as unclassified (the singleton classify job decides), subject only to the backfill limit. A refresh is incremental: a channel's newest 30 uploads until known ones are reached, a playlist's full listing, and only new videos are queued.
- **Schedules are per source:** once (the default: a lookup on add, then nothing), off, 1h, 6h, 1d or daily@HH:MM (UTC). Existing sources keep their `every` interval. After a run a scheduled source is pending again at its next time; once/off sources are done until triggered by hand.
- **"Refresh now" and "Look up again"** on each source under "Added from this phone" make the job due now at priority 500.
- **The old name still works:** jobs under `index/jobs/watch/` move to `jobs/refresh/` on first touch (state, due time, priority and schedule kept), and the `watch` key of control.json is read as refresh (the `refresh` key wins). The node record lists the stages `download` and `refresh`.
- The add form's schedule choices are once / 1d / 6h / 1h; an empty backfill means all (a playlist) or the newest 100 (a channel).
